## Title
Fidget Toy Box: Antistress Toys

## Short description (≤ 80 chars)

## Description (English)
Take a deep breath and play! Fidget Toy Box is a cozy shelf of 12 satisfying antistress toys:

- Pop It — press every bubble, then flip it over
- Fidget Spinner — flick it and chase your best RPM
- Bubble Wrap — endless sheets of crunchy bubbles
- Slime — poke, squish and stretch a jiggly glitter blob
- Switch Board — toggles, knobs, sliders and a big red button
- Xylophone — play freely or learn simple songs
- Kinetic Sand — slice layered rainbow sand
- Balloon Pop — pop floating balloons and catch the golden one
- Drum Pads — make beats with 6 different kits
- Spin Art — drip paint on a spinning disc and create art
- Zen Garden — rake calm patterns and arrange stones
- Newton's Cradle — classic clicking steel balls

Earn stars while you play and unlock 60 new styles: new shapes, colors, sounds and themes.
Relaxing ambient music, soft sounds, no timers, no pressure. Works with touch, mouse and keyboard
on phones, tablets and computers.
