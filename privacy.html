Fidget Toy Box — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026 · Developer: Mai Nhat Bien

Summary

Fidget Toy Box does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no chat, no tracking and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs in the iOS app.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (stars, unlocked styles, toy statistics and settings such as music, sound and vibration) is saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Web and YouTube versions

On the web version, progress is kept in your browser's local storage. When played inside YouTube Playables, progress is saved through YouTube's own cloud save and any ads are provided by YouTube under Google's privacy policy.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children or anyone else.
