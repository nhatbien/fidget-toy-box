Fidget Toy Box — Support

12 relaxing fidget toys to pop, spin, squish and play.

How to play

Pick a toy on the wooden shelf and play with it: tap, swipe, drag or hold. Every interaction fills the ★ meter. When you have enough stars the next style of a toy unlocks automatically — tap the card to try it. Tap the house button to go back to the shelf.

Settings

Tap the gear on the shelf to turn music, sound effects or vibration on and off. On iPhone the game also follows the silent switch.

Progress

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app resets it.
